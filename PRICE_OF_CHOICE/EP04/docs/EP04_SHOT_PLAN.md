## The four key moments
* **The scar.** Two macro shots: the double's bound inner left wrist with a diagonal stitched scar, then Alex's own forearm
  slides in beside it, the same way up, scars side by side at the same height. Both use one texture function (`scarTex()`),
  so they are identical to the pixel. White flash + sting on «как у меня» (13.30).
* **The broken phone.** It hits the floor on «пол» (43.02, thud + shake), the glass cracks on «треснул» (43.62), the chat keeps
  glowing under the cracks, glitches and dies (45.80). The threat continues on the hall mirror across the hallway.
* **Room 204.** On «найди комнату двести четыре» a 2-second flash insert: an unknown corridor door under a flickering tube,
  then its number plate «204» (white flash, sting, tube hum). Nothing else about the room is shown.
* **The sister.** The masked officer raises his phone; her photo fills most of the screen (a covert photo through a window,
  reading at her desk — no location detail), then the red message, then a voice message that plays as the VO delivers
  her line. Hard cut to black right after «дверь».
