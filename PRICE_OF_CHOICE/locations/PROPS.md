## Glass
Classic **faceted Soviet glass tumbler (гранёный стакан)**, filled to the brim with clear water. One and the same glass in S01_A,
S01_C, S03_A, S05_A, S07_E. Stands on the tiles at the threshold, slightly left of centre. After S07_E a little water is spilled
around it — keep that in later shots.

### UI language (series-wide)
- Dark messenger, sender **«Без номера» / «номер скрыт»**, grey avatar with «?».
- Cold accent `#8FB8FF` for timers, muted green `#6ECDAA` for money, red `#E8484D` only for threats.
- Font Inter (bundled in `assets/fonts/`, SIL OFL).
- Clock in the status bar: 02:47 → 02:50 across the episode; timer values are computed from the real elapsed time.
- Address shown: «ул. Лесная, 14, кв. 37» (fictional). Do **not** show room 204 in EP01.
